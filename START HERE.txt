THE GARDEN - V1 DEMO

To open it, keep this folder together and double-click index.html.
It runs locally in a browser. No account, installation, or internet connection is needed.

This copy starts with fictional Threads, generic food and movement ideas,
an unchecked Monthly Close list, and one fictional Workroom prompt.
Planner events, job opportunities, current work, session history, grocery lists,
and personal notes start empty.

Anything someone adds is saved by their browser on their own computer.
The Garden Tools page can export or import that browser's data.
The demo uses separate browser storage from the original Garden on this computer.

To share it, send the whole The-Garden-V1-Demo.zip file.
